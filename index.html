One Ticket Service: what it would take

Consolidating Kapture, Ticket Service Java and SRS into a single governing service. This page is the build inventory — what gets retired, what gets built, and what has to change around it. Effort and sequencing are sized separately.

Ashish Raj · PM, Service Tickets POD
01

The change

Today — five hops, three systems of record
Ticket creation→ Kapture→ Ticket Service Java→ SRS→ TAS→ CSP app
Proposed — one governing body
Ticket creation→ New Ticket Service→ TAS→ CSP app

The call-centre team gets a ticket-viewing and creation tool on Wiom Hub, our internal platform, reading and writing the same store — rather than a third-party CRM holding its own copy.

02

Why consolidate

What three services cost us today

  • Three state machines — "resolved" means something different in each
  • Three databases, none authoritative
  • Three sets of backend logic that have gone unchecked against each other
  • The systems do not talk effectively — data stops at boundaries nobody owns
  • Every new person has to learn all three before changing anything

What one service gives us

  • One store. Agent view, CSP app and customer app all read the same record
  • One logic engine — reopening, TAT and classification defined once
  • One state machine. A ticket in Resolved means the same to every reader
  • No sync layer, so no sync failures to detect, reconcile or explain
  • One identity — CSP and customer profiles read from our systems, not re-created

The number that should drive the plan. Ticket Service Java carries 592 lines of test against 23,369 lines of code. It is the largest of the three, the oldest, and holds 3,000+ references to Kapture — and almost nothing records what its correct behaviour is. Rebuilding it is not porting logic; it is re-deriving business rules from source, with no safety net telling us when we got one wrong. Behaviour extraction has to be a funded piece of work, not an assumption.

04

What needs to be built

Eight blocks. Three are new software, three are changes to things that already exist, and two are the operational work of getting there safely.

B1

New Ticket Service — the core

Build

One service holding the ticket record, its state machine and every rule currently split across SRS and Ticket Service Java.

Ticket domain + single state machineOne vocabulary replacing four; every transition defined once
One TAT / SLA enginePer-subtype deadlines on one clock, with one working-window definition
Classification & routingIssue type → subtype → executor, from one mapping table
Reopen lineage & chain scoringA reopen linked to its original, measured and scored as one problem
Verification & closureA check that can block a closure, with the customer as fallback
Escalation & timersFirst-response and breach handling, one ladder
Idempotency & dedupOne contact produces one ticket, however it arrives
Event publishingOutbound contracts to TAS, Quality OS and both apps

Wiom Hub — agent console

Build

The replacement for what Kapture does for the CX team, on our own platform and our own data. This is the largest single new build and the one most likely to be underestimated.

Scope risk. If Kapture also provides telephony — call pop-up, click-to-dial, recording — this block is materially larger than a ticket UI. That must be confirmed before this page becomes a plan.

05

Answer before approving

Four unknowns that could change the shape of this

  1. Does Kapture provide telephony? Call pop-up, click-to-dial or recording would make B3 a CTI programme rather than a ticket UI. This is the single biggest swing factor.
  2. What else does CX run in Kapture? SLA reporting, agent performance, QA scoring, other ticket types beyond service — none of it is visible in our repositories, and all of it needs a home.
  3. How much history has to migrate, and how far back? This sets the size of B6 and whether archive-only is acceptable for older records.
  4. Who owns the downstream changes? 41 files sit with five other teams. Without their commitment this cannot be scheduled, only hoped for.
